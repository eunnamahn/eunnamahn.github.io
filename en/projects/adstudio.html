Key decisions

1. If the product changes, it is not an ad

Why: Generative models can alter a product's shape or label while changing the background.

What changed: The original product is composited onto the generated background, checked for masked-region CLIP similarity ≥ 0.85, then reviewed by a person.

Result: All 4 representative cases passed.

2. Publish only what a person approved

Why: Posting generated results automatically could put out a wrong ad.

What changed: Only human-approved images become 13-second Shorts scheduled to YouTube.

Result: The team's 486 automated tests passed.

Limitations

No pixel-level comparison (SSIM) or blind human evaluation yet; both are listed as next steps in the report.
