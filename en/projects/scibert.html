Biomedical abstract sentence classification · BERT vs. SciBERT embeddings

Sep – Dec 2019 · Penn State IST 597 Foundations of Deep Learning

Most biomedical abstracts are unstructured, so readers cannot jump to the methods or results they need. Keeping the HSLN sentence classifier as is, we replaced only the word embeddings, from Word2Vec to BERT and SciBERT. Advised by Dr. C. Lee Giles.

Key decisions

1. Keep the model, change only the embedding

Why: To know what changed the score, change one variable at a time.

What changed: The classifier and data stayed fixed; only the embedding was swapped.

Result: Low vocabulary coverage (16% of dataset tokens vs. 68% for Word2Vec) was identified as the bottleneck.

Limitations

Single-run results; run-to-run variance was not measured.
