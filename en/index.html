SAVERSJul – Sep 2026
Team lead · team of 4

Evacuation guidance for disaster-vulnerable residents

K-Digital Training Hackathon 2026 · FastAPI, LangChain, AWS EC2

Official flood alerts send the same text to everyone, but residents who cannot judge or evacuate alone need personal guidance, with no app install and no constant location tracking.

  • Kept shelter filtering and walking routes (OSMnx / NetworkX) in deterministic rules, so the LLM only explains fixed results.
  • A claim-level verifier checks every guardian answer against the user's event log: 35/35 correct on a 40-case golden set, 30/30 runtime responses matched, 12/12 adversarial prompts blocked.
  • In a 52-person survey, evacuation intent rose from 3.2 to 8.3 (p<0.001); won the Minister of Employment and Labor Award (Encouragement Prize).
More
  • Measured refusal in both directions: over-refusal 3.6% (1/28), but 6/6 should-refuse controls were answered; both failures traced to lexical retrieval, not the verifier.
  • Contributions: built the claim-level verifier, the golden-set evaluation and the AWS/CI deployment; first-authored the paper.

BidMateJun – Jul 2026
Evaluation · team of 5

RAG Q&A and summarization for public RFP documents

LangChain, LangGraph, FAISS, KURE-v1, EXAONE 3.5

100 public RFPs in HWP and PDF; answers must cite the source and refuse unanswerable questions.

  • Tested 708 configurations on a 95-question golden set covering five question types, scored by Hit@k, MRR, refusal accuracy and RAGAS.
  • The final local stack (KURE-v1 + dense k=4 + EXAONE 3.5) beat the OpenAI baseline: MRR 0.65 → 0.977, refusal accuracy 0.91 → 1.0, latency 11.27 s → 3.6 s, at $0 per query.
More
  • Traced a Hit@k stuck at 0.3 to a bug in the golden set and fixed it, found that Ollama's default 4,096-token context was quietly cutting off evidence (raised to 8,192), and dropped a reranker that added 21 s of latency for no gain.
  • Contributions: ran the embedding comparison, designed the golden-set and RAGAS evaluation, and wrote the automated scoring scripts.

PillScopeApr – May 2026
Project manager · team of 5

Object detection for oral medication images

YOLOv11, RT-DETRv2, RF-DETR, COCO

56 look-alike pill classes, only 186 training images, a 51× class imbalance, and up to 4 pills per image.

  • Grouped near-duplicate shots into the same split to prevent leakage, then ran 7 staged experiments across 4 detectors.
  • Minority-class crop-and-paste grew the data from 186 to 1,026 images; YOLOv11l reached validation mAP@[0.75:0.95] 0.936, and a 3-model WBF ensemble lifted the Kaggle score from 0.851 to 0.9355.
More
  • Compositing web pill images onto the competition background raised mAP@[0.75:0.95] from 0.919 to 0.936 and made a never-detected rare class detectable.
  • YOLOv11l was ahead of RF-DETR v3 (0.882) and RT-DETRv2 (0.876).
  • Contributions: PM; built the COCO → YOLO pipeline, YOLOv11l training, DETR experiments and crop-and-paste augmentation.

Exact math model of water and gas inflow into multiple oil wells
