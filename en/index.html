Jul – Sep 2026

SAVERS · Evacuation Guidance for Disaster-Vulnerable Residents

Team Lead · PM · QA · Infra (team of 4) · K-Digital Training Hackathon 2026 · FastAPI, LangChain, OSMnx, NetworkX, AWS EC2

  • Problem: official flood alerts send the same text to everyone, but residents who cannot judge or evacuate alone need personal guidance, with no app install and no constant location tracking.
  • Contributions: owned the evaluation code (a 40-case golden set written independently from the spec, 12 adversarial regressions, the scoring script, the false-refusal probe) and the infrastructure code (real FCM sending, AWS deployment, CI); kept the decision records (ADRs); first-authored the paper.
  • Shelter choice and walking routes are computed by rule-based code (OSMnx / NetworkX), not the LLM; the LLM only explains results that are already fixed.
  • The verifier splits each answer into claims and checks them against the event log, with a pass bar of zero unsupported claims: 35/35 golden answers passed, and 12/12 adversarial prompts were blocked (8/12 before the scorer was hardened).
  • Tested refusal in both directions: wrongly refused only 1 of 28 valid questions (3.6%), but answered all 6 out-of-scope questions from unrelated evidence, a failure grounding scores miss; traced it to retrieval, not the verifier.
  • User study (n = 52, acquaintances, within-subject): action clarity 3.2 → 8.3 out of 10 (Wilcoxon p<0.001); "would leave within 5 minutes" 13% → 60%.
  • Minister of Employment and Labor Award (Encouragement Prize, tied 7th of 326 teams); first-author oral presentation at ACK 2026.
  • Limitation: the generator was an offline stub that copies evidence sentences at measurement time; the numbers must be re-measured with a real model.

Jun – Jul 2026

BidMate · RAG Q&A and Summarization for Public RFP Documents

Evaluation (team of 5) · LangChain, LangGraph, FAISS, KURE-v1, EXAONE 3.5

  • Problem: 100 public RFPs (HWP/PDF); answers must cite the source and refuse unanswerable questions.
  • Contributions: built the K-means golden set and preprocessing experiments, ran the embedding comparison, designed the RAGAS evaluation, and wrote the automated scoring scripts.
  • Picked representative documents with K-means for a 50-question golden set, then grew it to 95 questions (5 types, 19 RFPs) with type-specific scorers.
  • Compared 708 configurations on the same golden set in 3 stages: MRR 0.65 → 0.977, Hit@4 1.0, refusal accuracy 0.91 → 1.0 (19/19).
  • Replaced OpenAI with a free local stack (KURE-v1 + dense k=4 + EXAONE 3.5): mean latency 11.3 s → 3.6 s, no external API cost.
  • Found that a score stuck at 0.3 came from errors in the golden set, not the model; raised Ollama's 4,096-token context to 8,192 after finding it cut off evidence; removed a reranker that added 21 s of latency for no gain.
  • Limitation: the same golden set was used to choose and to evaluate the configuration, so scores on unseen questions may be lower.

AI Ad Studio · Ad Image and Copy Generation for Small Businesses

Team Lead · PM · Evaluation (team of 5) · SDXL, ControlNet, IP-Adapter, FastAPI, GCP L4

  • Problem: ad images must be remade for every time slot and channel without changing the product.
  • Contributions: PM for a 4-week sprint; defined the evaluation criteria; owned the final report.
  • Made product preservation the top metric: the original product is composited onto generated backgrounds, then checked with masked-region CLIP similarity ≥ 0.85 plus human review; 4/4 representative cases passed.
  • Team results: LCM-LoRA cut background generation from 17.47 s to 1.72 s (~10×), 5.97 s per image end to end on an L4; 486 automated tests passed; human-approved images become 13-s Shorts scheduled to YouTube.
  • Limitation: no pixel-level comparison (SSIM) or blind human evaluation yet; listed as next steps in the report.

Apr – May 2026

PillScope · Object Detection for Oral Medication Images

Team Lead · PM (team of 5) · YOLOv11, RT-DETRv2, RF-DETR, COCO

  • Problem: 56 look-alike pill classes, only 186 training images, a 51× class imbalance (153 vs. 3 annotations), and up to 4 pills per image.
  • Contributions: PM; built the COCO → YOLO pipeline, YOLOv11l training, DETR experiments and crop-and-paste augmentation.
  • Grouped near-duplicate shots into the same split to prevent train/validation leakage.
  • Minority-class crop-and-paste grew the data from 186 to 1,026 images (mAP@0.5 0.886 → 0.964); compositing web pill images onto the competition background made a never-detected rare class detectable.
  • Across 7 staged experiments with 4 models, YOLOv11l reached Kaggle mAP@[0.75:0.95] 0.936, ahead of RF-DETR (0.882) and RT-DETRv2 (0.883), up from a first submission of 0.851 via confidence tuning and WBF ensembling.

Fall 2019

Biomedical Abstract Sentence Classification · BERT vs. SciBERT Embeddings

Course project, Penn State IST 597 Foundations of Deep Learning · team of 2, equal contribution · advised by Dr. C. Lee Giles · TensorFlow

  • Problem: most biomedical abstracts are unstructured, so readers cannot jump straight to the methods or results they need.
  • Contributions: extracted BERT/SciBERT token embeddings from model checkpoints to replace Word2Vec (5.4M entries → 31K) in a hierarchical BiLSTM-CRF sentence classifier (HSLN), and ran all five conditions, including the baseline re-run.
  • On PubMed 20k RCT (5 classes), matched the re-run baseline (test accuracy 92.43% vs. 92.35%, single run) with a ~175× smaller embedding table; identified low vocabulary overlap (16% vs. 68% for Word2Vec) as the bottleneck.

Exact Formula for Water and Gas Inflow into Multiple Oil Wells

M.S. thesis · Advisor: Prof. Russell T. Johns · potential-function analytical solution (Laplace equation, superposition), numerical integration (Newton–Cotes)

  • Derived an exact formula, with no simulation needed, for how water and gas get pulled into producing oil wells when all three phases flow at once, and extended it to six well layouts, including injector–producer pairs and a five-spot pattern.
  • Gave a quick way to compute the critical oil rate before water or gas breaks through, showed that capillary pressure lowers it significantly, and proposed the solution as a benchmark for numerical simulators.
