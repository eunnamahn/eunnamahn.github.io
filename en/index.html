SAVERS · Evidence-grounded evacuation guidance

2026.07 – 2026.09

Team Lead · PM · QA · Infra (team of 4) · K-Digital Training Hackathon 2026

🏆 Minister's AwardLLM safetyFastAPILangChainNetworkXAWS EC2
35/35 golden set
30/30 runtime claims matched
12/12 adversarial blocked
3.2 → 8.3 evacuation intent (n=52)

A no-install mobile-web service that turns official flood alerts into personal evacuation guidance for residents who struggle to judge or evacuate alone.

Details
  • Shelter filtering and walking routes (OSMnx / NetworkX) are fixed by deterministic rules; the LLM only explains results that are already decided.
  • A claim-level verifier checks every guardian answer against the user's event log and blocks unmatched claims before sending. Pass criterion: zero unsupported claims.
  • Measured refusal in both directions: over-refusal 3.6% (1/28), but all 6 should-refuse controls were answered. Both failures traced to lexical retrieval, not the verifier.
  • In a 52-person survey, self-reported evacuation intent rose from 3.2 to 8.3 (47 up, 0 down, p<0.001); won the Minister of Employment and Labor Award (Excellence Prize).
  • Contributions: built the claim-level verifier, the golden-set evaluation and the AWS/CI deployment; first-authored the paper.

Question answering over 100 public RFPs in mixed HWP/PDF: answers must cite the source, and unanswerable questions must be refused.

Details
  • Tested 708 configurations on a 95-question golden set covering 5 question types (short answer, number, contract terms, summary, refusal), scored by Hit@k, MRR, refusal accuracy and RAGAS.
  • The final local stack (KURE-v1 + dense k=4 + EXAONE 3.5) beat the OpenAI baseline: MRR 0.65 → 0.977, Hit@k 0.73 → 1.0, refusal accuracy 0.91 → 1.0, latency 11.27 s → 3.6 s, at $0 per query.
  • Debugged the evaluation itself: fixed a golden-set bug that pinned Hit@k at 0.3, found Ollama's default 4,096-token context silently truncating evidence (raised to 8,192), and dropped a reranker that added no score but raised latency from 3.6 s to 24.3 s.
  • Contributions: built the hybrid retrieval, ran the embedding comparison, designed the evaluation, and built the RFP agents.

PillScope · Object detection for oral medication images

2026.04 – 2026.05

Project Manager (team of 5)

Computer visionYOLOv11RT-DETRv2RF-DETRCOCO
186 → 1,026 training images
val mAP@[.75:.95] 0.936
Kaggle 0.851 → 0.9355 (+8.5 pp)

56 look-alike pill classes, only 186 training images, a 51× class imbalance (153 vs. 3 annotations), and up to 4 pills per image.

Details
  • Grouped near-duplicate shots into the same split to prevent train/validation leakage, then ran 7 staged experiments across YOLOv8l, YOLOv11l, RT-DETRv2 and RF-DETR v3, scored by mAP@[0.75:0.95].
  • Minority-class crop-and-paste grew the data from 186 to 1,026 images (mAP@0.5 0.886 → 0.964).
  • Compositing web pill images onto the competition background raised mAP@[0.75:0.95] from 0.919 to 0.936 and made a never-detected rare class detectable.
  • YOLOv11l outperformed RF-DETR v3 (0.882) and RT-DETRv2 (0.876); a 3-model WBF ensemble lifted the Kaggle score from 0.851 to 0.9355.
  • Contributions: PM; built the COCO → YOLO pipeline, YOLOv11l training, DETR experiments and crop-and-paste augmentation.
